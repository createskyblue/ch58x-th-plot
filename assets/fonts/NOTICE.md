# Extracted SimSun

SimSun Regular, version 5.24.
© Copyright ZHONGYI Electronic Co. 2001, 2020, 2022.

Extracted at the user's request from face0 of the local Windows
`C:\Windows\Fonts\simsun.ttc` as a standalone TTF. Glyph outlines,
hinting programs, embedded bitmaps, character mappings and embedding
restrictions are retained. No subsetting or raster conversion was done.
The webpage uses the family alias `Wallpaper Song`.

Original font license text is preserved in `SimSun-LICENSE.txt` and in
